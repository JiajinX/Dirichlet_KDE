1. When doing the mean shift clustering, we first estimate a finite mixture from the data by the EM algorithm, then find the optimal bandwidth from the first derivative of the estimated finite mixture. What if the estimated finite mixture has components with alpha < 1.5?

Feb 27:
1. For the first derivative of MISE, the alpha of the finite mixture should be larger than 1.5; alpha can not equal 1.5.  
2. If the test mixture's alpha < 1.5, the first derivative of MISE will have some problems.
3. If the test mixture's alpha > 1.5, the boundary value (<=1.5) does not affect the finite mixture estimation.
4. We should test the mixture with alpha > 1.5 for the first derivative estimation. (So the mixture with components on the boundary can not be used in the test)
